What can I import?

EPUB, TXT and FB2 work without extra setup. PDF, MOBI and AZW3 use optional local import tools; availability is shown above. PDF must contain selectable text; OCR is not included.

Books use Foliotter’s reading layout. Original fonts, scripts, external resources, audio and video are not loaded. DRM and encrypted files are unsupported. Import errors leave your original file untouched.
